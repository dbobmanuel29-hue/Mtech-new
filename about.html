Who M-TECH is

A gadget store that answers questions before it sells anything.

M-TECH Premium Gadget Store is a technology business based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. We supply smartphones, laptops and accessories, and we buy, sell and swap phones for customers across the city.

The idea behind M-TECH is simple. Buying a phone or a laptop should not feel like a gamble. Most people do not need to know chipset names or benchmark scores — they need someone to translate the specification sheet into plain language and say clearly which device will serve them best for the money they have.

That is the service we try to provide on every enquiry: an honest read of what you actually need, a shortlist rather than a catalogue, and a straight answer about what a device will and will not do.

We are a local business. When you message the WhatsApp number on this site, you are talking to M-TECH in Port Harcourt — not a call centre, not an automated reply system, and not a reseller in another country.

Why customers choose M-TECH

Guidance first, transaction second.

There are plenty of places to buy a phone in Port Harcourt. What we try to do differently is make the decision itself easier.

  • Model-specific information. Every product on this site is a named model with its own photograph and description — no vague listings.
  • Honest comparisons. If a cheaper device does the job you described, we will say so.
  • Condition transparency. You are told plainly what condition a unit is in before you commit.
  • Clear trade-in reasoning. When we value a device we explain which factors moved the number.
  • One point of contact. The same WhatsApp line for enquiries, trade-ins and after-sales questions.
  • Local accountability. We are here, in Port Harcourt, and our reputation travels by word of mouth.
Local Port Harcourt service

Built for how technology is really used here.

Advice that ignores local reality is not advice. Power supply, network coverage, servicing availability and resale demand all shape what makes a good buy in Rivers State.

Battery matters more here

We weight battery capacity and charging speed heavily when recommending a phone, because a day without steady power is a normal day.

Serviceability counts

A device with locally available parts and technicians is worth more to you over three years than one that has to be shipped out for a simple repair.

Resale is part of the value

Most phones here get sold on. We factor future trade-in value into our advice, because what you recover later is part of what the phone truly costs.

Buying, selling, swapping

How a typical M-TECH conversation goes.

You message us

A model you are considering, a budget, or a device you want valued. One WhatsApp message is enough to begin.

We ask questions

How you use your phone, what frustrates you about the current one, what you are willing to spend. This is where the real work happens.

We shortlist

Two or three genuine options with the differences explained in plain language — including the cheaper option, if it fits.

You decide

Buy, sell, swap or think about it. No pressure and no follow-up harassment.

Product guidance & customer-first approach

What we will and will not do.

  • We will tell you when a previous-generation model is the smarter buy.
  • We will explain what a specification actually means for your daily use.
  • We will tell you if your current phone is still good enough and an upgrade can wait.
  • We will not describe a device as something it is not.
  • We will not quote a trade-in figure we cannot stand behind after inspection.
  • We will not pressure you into a decision you are not comfortable with.
