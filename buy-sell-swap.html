How device valuation works

Why two identical models can be worth different amounts.

Valuation is not a mystery. It comes down to a handful of factors, and we will tell you which ones are working for you and which are working against you.

Model & age

Newer and more in-demand models hold more value. Some older models stay strong locally because demand for them stays strong.

Storage capacity

Higher storage variants are usually worth more, especially on iPhone where storage cannot be expanded.

Battery health

A battery well below its original capacity means a replacement cost that has to be reflected in the offer.

Screen & body

Cracks, deep scratches, dents and previous repairs all affect the figure. Light wear is normal and expected.

Functionality

Cameras, speakers, microphones, buttons, charging port, Face ID or fingerprint — everything must work as it should.

Locks & status

The device must be fully unlocked from iCloud or Google, not network locked, and not reported lost or stolen.

What affects trade-in value

Things that raise it, things that lower it.

Raises your value

  • Original box, charger and cable still available
  • Screen free of cracks and deep scratches
  • Battery health still high
  • Higher storage capacity
  • Device has always been in a case with screen protection
  • No previous third-party repairs

Lowers your value

  • Cracked screen or back glass
  • Battery health significantly degraded
  • Water damage indicators triggered
  • Replaced screen or non-original parts
  • Faulty camera, speaker, microphone or charging port
  • Still signed in to iCloud or a Google account
Device condition checklist

Grade your own device before you message us.

Being accurate here saves everyone time — and an honest description is the fastest route to an offer that does not change when you arrive.

ExcellentLooks close to new. No cracks, no visible scratches, everything works, battery health still high.
GoodLight signs of use — small scuffs on the frame or fine screen marks — but no cracks and everything works.
FairClear wear, deeper scratches or dents. Screen intact and all functions still working.
Faulty / damagedCracked glass, failing battery, or a component not working. Still worth asking about — just tell us exactly what is wrong.
What to bring

Come prepared and it takes minutes.

  • The device, charged enough for us to check it properly
  • Charger and cable, plus the original box if you still have it
  • Any accessories that came with the device
  • Proof of ownership — receipt, box with matching IMEI, or original purchase record where available
  • A valid means of identification
  • Accounts signed out — iCloud / Google removed and screen lock disabled
  • Your data backed up and SIM plus memory card removed
