Choosing the right iPhone

Start with how you use your phone.

Four questions answer this faster than any spec sheet. Message us your answers and we will shortlist two models, not ten.

Do you shoot a lot?

If photos and video are the point of your phone, go Pro or Pro Max for the full camera system and zoom. If you mostly shoot for WhatsApp and Instagram, the standard iPhone 17 or iPhone 16 is genuinely enough.

How long is your day?

Heavy day, no charger, plenty of hotspot and navigation? Pro Max. Home or office within reach of a socket? The standard models will get you there comfortably.

Size and weight?

If a big phone tires your hand, look at iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 or iPhone Air. Air in particular is built around being light for its screen size.

How much storage?

128GB works if you back up regularly. If you keep years of photos and videos on the device, 256GB is the safer floor — and the harder thing to change later.

What is the budget?

iPhone 16 and iPhone 17e deliver most of the modern iPhone experience for less. Tell us your ceiling and we will show what it actually reaches.
