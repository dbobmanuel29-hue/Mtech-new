Galaxy Z foldables

Is a foldable right for you?

Foldables are genuinely different devices. Before you buy one, it helps to be honest about how you would use it.

  • Fold suits you if you read documents, run two apps side by side or watch a lot of video.
  • Flip suits you if you want a normal-size screen that disappears into a small bag or pocket.
  • Both need a little more care than a slab phone — a proper case is not optional.
  • Battery life is good but a Fold used as a mini tablet will drink more power.
  • Come and hold both if you can — five minutes decides it faster than any review.
