Protection first

The cheapest repair is the one you avoid.

A case and a sheet of tempered glass together cost a fraction of a screen replacement — and far less than the value your phone loses with a cracked display when you come to swap it.

  • Model-cut cases so buttons, ports and camera cutouts line up exactly
  • Raised lips around the screen and camera to survive face-down drops
  • Tempered glass in clear, matte, privacy and full-coverage options
  • Camera lens protectors for phones with large protruding camera modules
  • Fitting on request — dust-free, no bubbles

Power & charging

Charge properly, or charge slowly.

Fast charging is a three-part chain: the phone, the adapter and the cable. Break any link and you are back to overnight charging. We match all three.

  • Right wattage — 20W, 33W, 45W and 65W adapters for phones, tablets and slim laptops
  • Right standard — USB-C Power Delivery and Quick Charge, matched to your device
  • Cables that carry it — braided, high-current, reinforced ends
  • Power banks — 10,000mAh to 30,000mAh with honest capacity
  • Multi-device kits — one plug for phone, earbuds and watch

What to buy with a new phone
