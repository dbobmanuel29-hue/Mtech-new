Privacy Policy

This policy explains how M-TECH Premium Gadget Store handles customer information when you contact us, enquire about a product, buy a device, sell a device, or request a swap or trade-in valuation.

Last updated: 2026

M-TECH Premium Gadget Store is based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. The fastest way to contact us is WhatsApp: +234 907 311 2162.

1. Information we collect

We only collect information that helps us answer your enquiry or complete a transaction. This can include:

  • Your name and phone number.
  • Your email address, if you choose to provide it.
  • The product or service you are asking about.
  • Device information for valuations, including model, storage, condition, battery health, lock status and photographs you choose to send.
  • Identification or proof-of-ownership details where needed for a sale, purchase, swap or trade-in verification.

2. How forms work on this website

The contact and buy/sell/swap forms on this website do not save your details to a website database. They create a pre-filled WhatsApp message that you can review before sending to M-TECH.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries about phones, laptops, accessories, prices and availability.
  • To assess a device you want to sell or swap.
  • To verify ownership and reduce fraud risk during trade-in transactions.
  • To arrange collection, delivery or follow-up support where applicable.
  • To keep a basic transaction record where required for business and accountability purposes.

4. WhatsApp and third-party platforms

When you contact M-TECH through WhatsApp, your use of WhatsApp is governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy and terms. M-TECH does not control how WhatsApp processes data on its platform.

5. Sharing information

M-TECH does not sell customer information. We only share information when it is needed to complete a service you requested, comply with a lawful requirement, verify ownership, prevent fraud, or arrange delivery or collection.

6. Device data

Before selling or swapping a device, you are responsible for backing up your data and signing out of iCloud, Google or similar accounts. M-TECH recommends removing personal files, SIM cards and memory cards before handing over any device.

7. Security

We take reasonable care with information sent to us. However, no messaging platform or internet communication is completely risk-free. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information over chat.

8. How long we keep information

Enquiry details are kept only as long as needed to respond or follow up. Transaction details may be kept for business records, accountability and fraud-prevention purposes.

9. Your choices

You can ask M-TECH to correct or remove information you previously sent, subject to any transaction, fraud-prevention, ownership-verification or legal record that may need to be retained.

10. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when M-TECH changes how enquiries, sales or trade-ins are handled. The latest version will be published on this page.
