1. Product information

M-TECH aims to describe every product accurately. Each listing on this website names a specific model and is accompanied by a photograph and description of that model.

  • Photographs are representative of the model. Colour, finish, included accessories and packaging may vary between individual units.
  • Specifications listed are general summaries and may vary by market variant, storage configuration or manufacturer revision.
  • Where a device is pre-owned or previously used, its actual condition will be described to you directly before purchase.
  • If any detail matters to your decision — exact storage, colour, battery health, box contents or region variant — please confirm it with M-TECH before you commit.

2. Pricing

Prices are not published on this website. Gadget pricing in Nigeria changes with exchange rates, supply, and the condition and specification of the individual unit.

  • Current prices are confirmed directly by M-TECH, normally over WhatsApp or by phone.
  • A price quoted to you applies to the specific unit and configuration discussed, and may change if the item, specification or circumstances change.
  • Quotes may be given a validity period. Where that is the case, it will be stated at the time.
  • Accessory and service prices are quoted separately from device prices unless we state otherwise.

3. Availability

Stock changes frequently. A model appearing on this site indicates that it is part of the range M-TECH deals in, not that a unit is in stock at that moment.

  • Availability is confirmed at the time of enquiry.
  • Some models can be sourced to order; timescales will be discussed with you where that applies.
  • Where a specific unit has been reserved for you, any agreed reservation arrangement will be confirmed directly.

4. Buying from M-TECH

A sale is agreed when you and M-TECH have confirmed the device, its specification, its condition and the price, and payment has been completed in line with what was agreed.

  • Please inspect and test the device at the point of collection or delivery.
  • Confirm that the model, storage, colour and condition match what was agreed before completing the transaction.
  • Where box contents are relevant (charger, cable, documentation), these are confirmed at the point of sale.

5. Selling to M-TECH

You may sell a device to M-TECH subject to assessment and agreement.

  • Any figure given before physical inspection is indicative and based on the description you provide.
  • The final offer is confirmed after M-TECH has physically inspected and tested the device.
  • You must be the lawful owner of the device, or be authorised to sell it.
  • Devices must be fully signed out of iCloud, Google or any similar account, must not be network locked unless disclosed and agreed, and must not be reported lost or stolen.
  • M-TECH may request proof of ownership and a valid means of identification.
  • M-TECH may decline to purchase any device at its discretion.

6. Swapping and trade-ins

A swap places the assessed value of your device against the price of the device you want, with the balance settled by whichever party owes it.

  • Both values are explained to you before the swap is agreed.
  • All conditions applying to selling a device to M-TECH (section 5) apply equally to swaps.
  • Once a swap is completed and both devices have changed hands, it is treated as final unless otherwise agreed in writing.
  • You are responsible for backing up and removing your data before handing over a device.

7. Device condition

Condition is assessed on the physical state and full functionality of a device.

  • Factors assessed include screen and body condition, battery health, camera, speakers, microphones, buttons, charging port, biometrics and connectivity.
  • Water damage indicators, previous repairs and non-original parts are taken into account.
  • Where a device you present differs from the description you gave, M-TECH will explain the difference and may revise the offer accordingly.
  • Pre-owned devices sold by M-TECH are described honestly, including any cosmetic wear.

8. Payments

  • Accepted payment methods are confirmed at the time of the transaction.
  • Ownership of a device passes to the buyer once payment has been received in full and the transaction is complete.
  • Please retain any receipt or written confirmation issued to you.
  • M-TECH will never ask you to send payment to an account other than one it has confirmed to you directly. If you receive an unexpected payment request claiming to be from M-TECH, verify it on +234 907 311 2162 before sending anything.

9. Delivery and collection

  • Collection and delivery arrangements are agreed case by case, depending on your location and the item.
  • Where a third-party courier or dispatch service is used, delivery timelines are estimates and are subject to that provider.
  • Risk in a device passes on collection or on delivery to the address or person you nominate.
  • Please check the item in the presence of the person handing it over wherever possible.

10. Returns

Returns are handled case by case and depend on the item, its condition and the circumstances.

  • If a device is not as described, contact M-TECH as soon as possible so it can be resolved.
  • Any return will require the item to be in the same condition it was supplied in, with the accessories it was supplied with.
  • Certain items — for example fitted screen protection or opened consumable accessories — may not be returnable for hygiene or practical reasons.
  • Nothing in these terms is intended to remove rights you may have under applicable Nigerian consumer law.

11. Warranty

  • Where a manufacturer warranty applies to a new device, that warranty is provided by the manufacturer under its own terms.
  • Any additional cover M-TECH offers on a specific item will be stated to you at the point of sale, including what it covers and for how long.
  • Warranty cover does not extend to accidental damage, liquid damage, misuse, or damage caused by third-party repairs or non-approved parts, unless expressly stated.
  • Pre-owned devices may be sold with limited or no warranty; this is made clear before purchase.

  • Any information you send to M-TECH by WhatsApp, phone or in person is used to respond to your enquiry and complete your transaction.
  • Identification details requested for a device purchase or trade-in are collected for verification and record-keeping purposes.
  • M-TECH does not sell customer information to third parties.
